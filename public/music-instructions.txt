Music Instructions:
==================

To add background music to your portfolio:

1. Place your music file (MP3 format) in this public folder
2. Rename it to "background-music.mp3" 
3. The MusicPlayer component will automatically detect and play it

Recommended music types:
- Soft instrumental music
- Lo-fi beats
- Ambient background music
- Make sure the file is not too large (under 5MB recommended)

The music player includes:
- Play/Pause button
- Volume control
- Mute button
- Auto-loop functionality
